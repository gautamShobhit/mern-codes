Main Objective

Imagine you are starting a business. Design, structure and style its landing website from scratch. This tests your HTML and CSS to the core. No restrictions!

What You Will Implement

Pick any business (cafe, gym, tuition centre, clothing brand, travel agency...) and build a landing page with:

-> Hero section with background image/colour, headline, and a call-to-action button. Use position and z-index (for example, text over a dark overlay).
-> About / Services section with 3 card variants (different styles using border-radius, backgrounds, shadows).
-> Gallery or Testimonials section with images.
-> Contact section (can reuse your form skills).
-> Footer with links.
-> Concepts you must use: box model (margin, padding, border), positioning, background styling, borders, images, overflow.
-> Bonus: annotate a screenshot of one element showing its content, padding, border and margin (save as box-model.png).

Note: Layout will be done with normal flow and positioning for now. Flexbox and Grid are coming in the next assignments!
