Main Objective

Get comfortable with one-dimensional layouts using Flexbox.

What You Will Implement

Make four small pages in one folder:

-> menu.html: a simple horizontal menu bar using display: flex.
-> login.html: a login form perfectly centred on the screen (horizontally and vertically).
-> products.html: a product listing of at least 8 items using flex-wrap.
-> navbar-hero.html: a navbar + hero section made with Flexbox (logo on the left, links on the right, hero text centred).

Use: justify-content, align-items, flex-direction, flex-wrap, gap, flex-grow.
